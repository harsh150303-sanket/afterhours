PRIVACY NOTICE

Your chats and media

AfterHours processes and may store messages, photos, and videos so the chat can work and conversation history can be shown. The service operator may access stored chats and media. Do not send anything you would not want the service operator to be able to access.

View-once is intended to remove media from normal chat storage after the recipient opens it. It cannot prevent screenshots, screen recording, external recording, cached copies, or copies made before opening. Do not treat it as end-to-end encryption.

Chat history persistence depends on the configured database. If storage is not configured, history may be lost when the service restarts. Avoid sending highly sensitive personal information.
